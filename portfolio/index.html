FAQ

Common questions

How much does a project cost?
It depends on the size of the job. Send me a short description of what you need and I'll reply with a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.
How long does delivery take?
Small tasks like data cleaning usually take 1–3 days. Dashboards and larger analyses usually take 1–2 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes. Every project includes revisions so the final result matches what you need.
Is my data kept private?
Yes. Your data is only used for your project, never shared, and deleted when we finish if you prefer.
What do you need from me to get started?
A short description of your goal and a sample of your data (Excel, CSV or database access). I'll take it from there.
